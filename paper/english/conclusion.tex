\section{Conclusion}
OpenVisualJev combines tree-masked multimodal encoding, permutation-equivariant set interaction, and calibrated dynamic pointer scoring. Controlled experiments establish decision stability and candidate-dependent comparison, while recognition and VQA tests assess standard task capability. Variable-count training and probability calibration further improve robustness and decision scores. These results support explicit candidate-set modeling as a practical design for multimodal decision systems.

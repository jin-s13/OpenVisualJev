\section{Experimental setup}
All methods use Qwen3-VL-4B-Instruct~\citep{bai_2025_qwen3vl}, frozen vision parameters, and LoRA on language attention projections~\citep{hu_2021_lora}. Set Pointer and the complete-candidate LM are trained with AdamW~\citep{loshchilov_2017_adamw}, cross-entropy, and the dataset-wise calibration loss. Independent Pointer additionally tests candidate-independent scoring in the relation task. Section~\ref{sec:order-stability} defines the interfaces.

The reported accuracy and probability tables were evaluated with ordinary batched branches using the same branch visibility and positional coordinates described in the method.

\paragraph{Architecture and scoring.}
The backbone hidden width is 2560. Candidate summaries are projected to width 256; Set uses two pre-normalized position-free Transformer blocks, four attention heads, feed-forward expansion four, GELU, and zero dropout. Independent removes cross-candidate blocks. The separate question query bypasses candidate attention. The readout operates in single precision over bfloat16 backbone features. The LM scores the next-token logits of single-token candidate symbols, directly normalized over the supplied alternatives. Pointer branches omit candidate indices. Pointer uses separate candidate contexts, while LM uses one complete-candidate context.

\paragraph{Unified recognition.}
One checkpoint per method covers Pets~\citep{parkhi_2012_pets}, CUB, Cars~\citep{krause_2013_cars}, Dogs, Flowers~\citep{nilsback_2008_flowers}, and Aircraft~\citep{maji_2013_aircraft}. Training, development, and calibration contain 31,088, 3,637, and 3,635 images. Testing uses 35,566 FOCI-style four-choice records~\citep{geigle_2024_foci} released with Finedefics~\citep{he_2025_finedefics}, preserving its repeated Dogs row and shuffling candidates identically across methods.

\paragraph{Data and candidate construction.}
Development and calibration images are reserved from official training-side data, using the official validation images for Flowers and Aircraft. Exact and reviewed near-duplicate image groups are separated from the test inventory, with cross-dataset exclusions before mixed training. The six datasets contain 37, 200, 196, 120, 102, and 100 classes, respectively. Each classification record supplies a smaller candidate set containing the gold class. Standard testing preserves the released Finedefics candidates. For difficult-candidate transfer, incorrect classes are selected by a fixed OpenCLIP ViT-L-14 image--text ranking, so each model receives the same distractors at each candidate count.

\paragraph{Main model and component ablation.}
The complete models use balanced hard-four-choice training: 96 questions per effective batch, 16 per dataset, seed 20261021, and the epoch-three checkpoint after 2,016 updates. Both receive identical candidate exposures, calibration-loss weight five, and separately fitted temperatures minimizing equal-dataset calibration NLL. Table~\ref{tab:training-calibration} ablates the training penalty and temperature under this recipe.
\label{sec:training-calibration-protocol}

The learning rate is 0.0001 with 61 warmup updates, cosine decay, AdamW weight decay 0.01, gradient clipping at norm one, and language-attention LoRA rank/scaling 16/32. Training comprises 193,536 question exposures. The calibration coefficient of five is the evaluated development setting; the balanced recipe was developed after inspecting earlier benchmark results. The CE control sets this coefficient to zero, retaining the same data, updates, and final-epoch selection. Each model fits a positive scalar temperature on the reserved calibration split before standard testing.

\paragraph{Natural VQA.}
Both interfaces are separately adapted to A-OKVQA~\citep{schwenk_2022_aokvqa} across three seeds and evaluated on 2,576 internal holdout questions. Training, selection, and calibration roles are disjoint.

\paragraph{VQA splits and training.}
A-OKVQA training uses 12769 questions over 12404 images; selection uses 859 questions over 827 images, and calibration uses 852 questions over 827 images. The internal holdout has 2576 questions over 2482 images, grouped into 2481 resampling components after a reviewed near-duplicate merge. It is split from the official training inventory. Seeds are 20260924, 20260927, and 20260930. Models train for three epochs with learning rate 0.0002 and accumulation over eight questions; LoRA, optimizer, warmup, and clipping settings otherwise follow the shared recipe above. Checkpoint selection maximizes answer-text correctness, then minimizes NLL, then prefers the earlier epoch. Blank and duplicate choices are retained; probability mass for duplicate literal answer strings is summed when computing answer-event losses. Images are uncropped.

\paragraph{Metrics.} We report accuracy, NLL, sum-Brier, and ECE with held-out temperature calibration~\citep{guo_2017_calibration}. Recognition weights datasets equally. Paired intervals resample image clusters for recognition and scenes for relations (Appendix~\ref{app:essential}). Bold marks best point estimates, including displayed ties.

\paragraph{Selection and uncertainty.}
Complete-method comparisons use the fixed third-epoch checkpoints. The separate CE candidate-count ablation selects checkpoints by equal-dataset development accuracy, preferring the earlier epoch on ties. Calibration and test examples have separate roles, and fitted temperatures remain frozen during testing. Recognition intervals use 10,000 paired bootstrap replicates within datasets, retaining repeated image rows in the same cluster and averaging datasets equally. Relation intervals resample complete scenes to preserve paired questions. VQA intervals resample images and the three training seeds and are exploratory. Intervals condition on the selected weights and fitted temperatures; they describe evaluation-sample uncertainty rather than variation over retraining or calibration refitting. All reported intervals are marginal, without multiplicity correction.

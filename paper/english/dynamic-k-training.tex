\subsection{Training with variable candidate counts}
\label{sec:dynamic-k-training}
Fixed four-choice and variable-$K$ training ($K\in\{2,4,10\}$, uniformly sampled) share image exposure, updates, development selection, and test sets. Candidate-token computation varies; twenty-choice tests exceed the training range.

\centering\small
\begin{tabular}{lrrrr}
\toprule
Test & LM fixed & Set fixed & LM variable & Set variable \\
\midrule
Standard & 89.08 & 89.79 & 89.08 & \textbf{89.82} \\
Hard 2 & 92.87 & \textbf{93.36} & 92.73 & 93.26 \\
Hard 4 & 89.25 & \textbf{89.93} & 89.13 & 89.91 \\
Hard 10 & 84.94 & 86.27 & 86.02 & \textbf{87.24} \\
Hard 20 & 82.01 & 84.47 & 84.20 & \textbf{85.82} \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\caption{Equal-dataset accuracy (\%) for fixed four-candidate training and variable-candidate training. All four models use the same single training seed and test records. Difficult-distractor tests and the author-defined standard test are separate constructions.}
\label{tab:dynamic-k-training}
\end{table}
Variable-$K$ training improves twenty-choice accuracy by 2.19 points for LM and 1.35 for Set. Set retains a 1.62-point lead (paired interval $[1.31,1.93]$) and perfect tested order stability; standard accuracy changes little.

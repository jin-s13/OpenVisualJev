\section{Results and analysis}
\subsection{Order stability}
\label{sec:order-stability}
We compare three interfaces to test candidate-order stability (Table~\ref{tab:structural-properties}).

\textbf{Independent Pointer} scores isolated candidate representations with a shared 256-dimensional query--key pointer. \textbf{Set Pointer} adds two position-free Transformer blocks (four heads, fourfold feed-forward expansion, GELU, zero dropout); the query bypasses interaction. \textbf{Complete-candidate LM} jointly encodes labeled alternatives and normalizes next-token logits over their single-token labels. All directly output candidate probabilities.

On 755 questions, we evaluate all 24 candidate permutations and align predictions by candidate identity. LM changes its answer on 108 questions (14.30\%); Set changes on none. Figure~\ref{fig:decision-analysis} summarizes order stability and difficult-candidate transfer for the complete-method checkpoints.

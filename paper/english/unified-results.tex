\paragraph{Training-time calibration.}
Under the same balanced recipe, the training penalty improves raw NLL, Brier, and ECE (Table~\ref{tab:training-calibration}). Combining it with temperature scaling further reduces NLL and Brier relative to temperature-scaled CE training. The NLL difference is $-0.01539$ (paired interval $[-0.01953,-0.01142]$), with a small accuracy change of $-0.1273$ points. Appendix~\ref{sec:training-calibration-protocol} gives the recipe.

\section*{AI usage disclosure}
We used generative AI tools throughout the research and writing workflow, under human direction. The writing workflow used the open-source \href{https://github.com/jin-s13/ai-research-writing-skill}{\texttt{ai-research-writing-skill}} to organize manuscript revision, evidence checks, and document validation. Assistance included literature search and synthesis; refinement of research questions, hypotheses, and the conceptual framework; formulation and exposition of mathematical properties; and design of experiments and statistical analyses. These tools also assisted with implementing model components, training and evaluation pipelines, data cleaning and reformatting, experiment orchestration, debugging, and result interpretation.

AI assistance contributed to the design and implementation of the procedural synthetic-scene generator used in the relation experiments. The resulting images, candidate sets, and labels were produced by executable generation code. Generative AI also assisted with creating the method illustration, drafting and restructuring the manuscript, English--Chinese translation, language editing, reference formatting, and document production.

The reported empirical results are computed from model predictions using the recorded evaluation and analysis scripts. Verification in the workflow includes checkpoint reload comparisons, data-manifest and artifact checks, numerical-evidence checks, citation checks, and inspection of rendered documents. The human author defines the research objectives, makes methodological and editorial decisions, and reviews the manuscript and supporting evidence. The author retains responsibility for the final text, claims, code, data, and figures, including AI-assisted contributions.

\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}
\paragraph{Multimodal decision models.}
Visual Jev and PixelJev use option-token readouts~\citep{yu_2026_visualjev,zhou_2026_pixeljev}; Koa-action learns atomic labels under randomized mappings~\citep{dai_2026_koa}. OmniJev combines learned scores, probabilistic supervision, and calibration~\citep{xu_2026_omnijev}, while Chinese-Jev uses a text encoder and heterogeneous supervision~\citep{wang_2026_chinesejev}. Visual Jev shares image prefixes, and FastJEV studies state reuse and layer pruning~\citep{yu_2026_visualjev,ma_2026_fastjev}. OpenVisualJev combines isolated candidate grounding with permutation-equivariant joint comparison.

\paragraph{Set-dependent choice.}
Deep Sets and Set Transformer provide invariant and equivariant representations with learned interactions~\citep{zaheer_2017_deepsets,lee_2019_set}. FETA and FATE model utilities that depend on the surrounding alternatives~\citep{pfannschmidt_2019_context}. We apply these principles to multimodal candidates and test both preference reversals and order stability.

\paragraph{Probability quality and decision evaluation.}
Decision benchmarks examine calibration, selective prediction, ordering, and candidate counts~\citep{deusser_2026_benchmark,rafe_2026_gates}; routing, security, and mobile agents use such scores downstream~\citep{li_2026_jevjudge,liu_2026_security,zhang_2026_jevmobile}. Visual MCQA debiasing and selective VQA study related reliability issues~\citep{atabuzzaman_2025_bias,eisenschlos_2024_selective}. Our probability pipeline combines confidence--accuracy regularization~\citep{liang_2020_dca,hebbalaguppe_2022_mdca} with temperature scaling~\citep{guo_2017_calibration}.

\paragraph{Fine-grained visual recognition.}
FOCI casts fine-grained recognition as multiple-choice prediction~\citep{geigle_2024_foci}; Finedefics combines attribute alignment with classification instruction tuning~\citep{he_2025_finedefics}. We reuse its released candidate records to compare unified Qwen3-VL scoring architectures across six datasets.
